undertale-3ds

An unofficial fan port of UNDERTALE to the Nintendo 3DS. You build it from your own copy of UNDERTALE.

View the project on GitHub

Status

The UNDERTALE 1.0 beta is not out yet. The goal is a locked 30 fps in every scene on every model. Current progress, changes and known issues are on the GitHub page, updated about once a day.

DELTARUNE comes after UNDERTALE.

Supported consoles

  • New 3DS, New 3DS XL, New 2DS XL
  • 3DS, 3DS XL, 2DS

What you need

  • UNDERTALE 1.08 for PC, from Steam or GOG
  • A Windows, Linux or Mac computer
  • A 3DS that can run homebrew, and an SD card

How it works

A small builder app on your computer finds your copy of UNDERTALE and builds a .3dsx or .cia for your console. It can also bring your PC save over. Nothing from the game is in this project, and the builder only works with a copy you own. Please don't share the files it makes.

Who made it

Hi, I'm Ethan, a Computer Science student at Western University. Most of the code was written with AI models (mainly Claude) under my supervision and direction, and every room, font and sprite was checked against the original. Say hi on Discord: 3lit3hax.
